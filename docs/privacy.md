# Jimmy Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026.

Jimmy is a podcast player for iPhone. It is built so that your data stays yours: the developer does not
collect, see or sell any of it.

## What Jimmy stores, and where

- **Your subscriptions, queue, playback progress, bookmarks and listening stats** are stored on your device in the
  app's private container.
- **Downloaded episodes** are files in the app's private container.

## iCloud sync (optional)

When **iCloud Sync** is on in Settings, Jimmy saves a small sync record to your own iCloud key-value store so your other
devices can pick it up: the feed URLs you subscribe to, the order of your queue, playback positions and which
episodes are played. It goes through Apple's iCloud under your Apple Account; the developer cannot read it. Turn it
off in Settings to stop syncing.

## What leaves your device

- **Podcast feeds, artwork and audio** are loaded directly from each podcast's host, which has its own privacy policy.
- **Discover and search** send your search terms to Apple (iTunes Search API and Apple's podcast charts).
- **The feedback form** sends the name and notes you type, and only when you tap Send.
- Nothing else. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

## On-device AI (planned)

The planned chat and suggestions use Apple's on-device model only. Your requests and listening history are not sent
anywhere.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass crash and performance reports
to the developer through Xcode Organizer and MetricKit. They contain no library content.

## Deleting your data

Delete Jimmy to remove all data it stored on your device. Downloaded episodes are deleted with the app.

## Children

Jimmy is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
